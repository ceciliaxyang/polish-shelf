Pill icon animations

Hover a pill to play its animation (tap on a phone). Each icon has a few options, lettered. Reply with your picks, like “Magnetic B, Jelly A, Mooncat A”, or “none” for any icon that should stay still.

All are CSS/SVG only and run once per hover, 0.4 to 1s. On the live site they’ll be off for anyone with Reduce Motion turned on.
